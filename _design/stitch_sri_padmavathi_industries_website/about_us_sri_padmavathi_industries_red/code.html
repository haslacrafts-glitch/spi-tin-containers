The Journey

Crafting Excellence in Chennai

Founded in 2009 in the industrial heart of Tamil Nadu, Sri Padmavathi Industries began with a singular vision: to redefine the standards of industrial packaging through technical precision and unwavering quality. Operating as a Sole Proprietorship, we have evolved into a premier manufacturer and wholesaler of high-grade Tin Containers, Ghee Tins, and Food Cans.

Under the visionary leadership of Mr. R Sugumar, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for diverse industries, ranging from food processing to luxury gifting. Our commitment to utilizing superior raw materials and state-of-the-art lithography ensures that every product leaving our facility is a testament to durability and design.
